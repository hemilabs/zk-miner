The HemiProve stack deployed on Hemi testnet 743111 on 2026-11-16: github.com/max-sanchez/hemi-prove-contracts
@ 8e1808b (main; the same files as hemilabs/hemiprove-contracts master 3bc441d apart from LICENSE).

How these files were produced (B9-CHAIN, 2026-11-17; docs/metal-port-notes/B9-CHAIN.md): the contracts repo was not
readable from the build host (private, no credentials), so the ABIs of the contracts deployed on 743111 were taken
from the Hemi testnet explorer's verified sources (testnet.explorer.hemi.xyz, api/v2/smart-contracts/<address>; every
contract verified, solc 0.8.38) and put in forge's `abi/*.abi.json` layout by a canonicaliser that reproduces the
previous c8333aa files byte for byte from the explorer ABIs of the 8 contracts whose ABI did not change.
HemiProveRouter.abi.json is the union of HemiProve and its facets keyed by (type, signature), first occurrence in the
order HemiProve, Core, Fulfill, FulfillCycle, Aux, AuxGov (the rule that reproduces the c8333aa file byte for byte).

Changed from c8333aa: HemiProve, HemiProveCore, HemiProveFulfill, HemiProveAux, HemiProveFulfillCycle (jobLaxCallbackMode
removed), HemiProveAuxGov (also markFormerBurnDestination removed, error ZeroEwmaKey added), HemiProveRouter (their
union), ProgramRegistry, BatchCycleCommittee.
Unchanged (byte-identical to the explorer's): HemiProveStaking, HemiProveRegistry, the three adapter ABIs (the RISC Zero,
SP1 Groth16 and SP1 PLONK adapters), ProofResultStore, GovernanceTimelock, GuardianPauseRelay.
Still c8333aa, not deployed or not checkable this way: HashChainConsumer, IHemiProveCallback, MockERC20 (the live
TestHemiToken's ABI is a superset of its ERC-20 part), MockRiscZeroVerifier, MockSP1Verifier,
OpenVmHalo2VerifierAdapter, and the two documents INTERACTION_GUIDE.md and CHANGELOG_ABI.md (their 2026-11-16
editions could not be fetched; the handoff of 2026-11-16 is the prover-facing summary until they are re-vendored).

To re-vendor from the repo: copy abi/*.abi.json and the two .md files from a checkout at 8e1808b; the tests in
crates/zkminer-contracts and crates/zkminer-chain/tests/abi_pins.rs then pin zkminer against them, and
`ZKMINER_CONTRACTS_REPO=<checkout> cargo test -p zkminer-chain --lib the_pins_match_the_contracts_repo` checks the code
pins against script/lib/ReleaseCorePins.sol and VerifierPins.sol.
