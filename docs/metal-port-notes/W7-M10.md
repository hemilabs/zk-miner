## 1. Budget enforcement (S3 b)

### Design

- `metal_host::apply_planned_budget` runs at worker start, after the tracing init and the support check, and before any HAL, graph preload, prewarm or proving thread exists.
- It computes the plan (`working_set_limit`: the smallest of recommended, an explicit budget and the figure). It then exports `RISC0_METAL_MAX_WORKING_SET_BYTES = plan` when `metal_sizing::enforced_budget` says so.
- The fork reads the variable when each HAL runtime is created (`runtime.rs:649`) and at each Groth16 call (`groth16-sys working_set_budget`). The worker drops its provers after every proof (M8 release plus the wrap's `release_hal_buffers`). So a worker's budget is fixed at its start, and a tier change is a respawn with another figure (section 2). No `setenv` happens while threads run.
- risc0 has no API for this. `MetalHal::set_working_set_limit` is per HAL, and zkvm builds its HALs internally.

`enforced_budget(plan, hal_limit, off)`:
- It exports only when the plan is at least a whole GiB under the HAL's limit (recommended, or an explicit budget if smaller).
- The table rows, every measured budget and every risc0 threshold sit on whole GiB, so a smaller gap is only the host reserve's rounding against macOS's own reserve.
- An explicit budget equal to the plan (gpu-run's, an operator's) is left alone.

Why enforcement loses:
- The fork's pool cap is half the limit (`runtime.rs:655`). At 8 GiB the cap is 4 GiB, under po2 19's 4.78 GiB segment working set, so the pools free and re-allocate buffers every segment. At 10.67 GiB the cap is 5.35 GiB and they don't.
- Freed Metal pages stay in the process footprint for seconds (M9 open issue 2). So the churn costs time and also raises the footprint peak, even though `currentAllocatedSize` peaks lower.
- The tier's footprint saving comes from its po2 (section 2), not from the budget.

**Decision:**
- Enforcement is off by default (`ZKMINER_METAL_ENFORCE_BUDGET=1` turns it on). This is the reverse of the brief's knob polarity, because the measured default must be the winner.
- The mechanism, its logs and its tests stay, so that a risc0 change can re-measure it. Such a change would be a pool cap not tied to the limit, or a purgeable return of freed pages.
- The worker now logs its plan against the HAL's limit at every start, e.g. `Metal budget: planned 8.00 GiB, not enforced (... the HAL's limit is 10.67 GiB)`.

## 2. Owner-active tier (small-mac-owner-active-tier)

### Activity sources (`discovery.rs`, trait `OwnerSource`, real one `MacOwnerSource`; no sudo, no entitlement)

| signal | source | this Mac |
|---|---|---|
| time since the last keyboard / mouse / trackpad input | IOKit `IOServiceGetMatchingService("IOHIDSystem")` -> `HIDIdleTime` (ns; CFNumber or CFData) | 155531 s (nobody at the console) |
| display asleep | CoreGraphics `CGDisplayIsAsleep(CGMainDisplayID())` | unknown (`CGMainDisplayID` is 0 in this session) |
| on battery | IOKit `IOPSGetProvidingPowerSourceType`, as M9's power guard | no |
| Low Power Mode | `NSProcessInfo.isLowPowerModeEnabled` via the ObjC runtime, as M9's power guard | no |

- One reading costs 56 us (debug build, 100 readings).
- Thermal state (the third part of M9's power state) is logged by the worker but does not move the tier: a smaller tier does not run cooler.

### Policy and hysteresis (`metal_sizing::OwnerTierState`)

1. Display asleep: idle at once.
2. Input within `ZKMINER_OWNER_IDLE_SECS` (default 300 s, macOS's screen-saver delay), on battery, or Low Power Mode: active at once.
3. From active back to idle only after the input has been idle for **twice** the threshold, with neither power signal set. Between one and two thresholds the tier stays where it was. That is the hysteresis band.
4. Nothing readable: no change (idle at start, which is today's behaviour).
5. `ZKMINER_OWNER_ACTIVE`: unset or `0` (the default since fix round 1) keeps the idle tier; `auto` runs this policy; `1` forces the active tier (tests, simulation).
6. A display asleep wins over battery and Low Power Mode, so a closed-lid laptop on battery counts as idle: the tier sizes memory for the owner's apps, none are in use, and battery drain is not this tier's concern.

Test `a_flapping_owner_switches_the_tier_rarely`: an owner who touches the mouse every 6 minutes for 8 hours causes 1 switch, against a switch every 6 minutes without the band.

### Tier table (derived, no new RAM fraction)

The idle figure is unchanged: `hw.memsize` less the 8 GiB host reserve (`metal_sizing::HOST_RESERVE_BYTES`, the same constant as `dispatcher::METAL_HOST_RESERVE_BYTES`). `active_limit` is the idle figure less that same reserve again ("the owner's apps get as much again as macOS keeps"), floored at the table's smallest row (the bottom of the lowest default row, 4 GiB, in either table) and never above the idle figure. The po2 is the M8 table's for that limit.

| Mac (`hw.memsize`) | idle | active | PLAN 9.1 budget, active / idle |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 GB | 8 GiB, po2 19 | **4 GiB, po2 18** | ~4 GB / ~7-8 GB |
| 18 GB | 10 GiB, po2 19 | 4 GiB, po2 18 | - |
| 24 GB | 16 GiB, po2 20 | 8 GiB, po2 19 | 6-12 GB / 12-28 GB |
| 36 GB | 28 GiB, po2 20 | 20 GiB, po2 20 | 16-64 GB / 40-180+ GB |

Pinned by `metal_sizing::the_active_tier_per_mac_size`, `dispatcher::a_macs_risc0_slot_figure_follows_the_owner_tier` (16/18/24/36 GB through `risc0_slots`) and `run.rs::a_macs_risc0_slot_takes_the_owner_active_tier`.

Since fix round 1 the claim side reports the active figure only where it claims at a smaller po2 (`metal_sizing::claim_limit`): 16 / 18 / 24 GB. A 36 GB Mac's active tier claims at po2 20 like its idle one and reports the idle figure, so it moves nothing.

### How a job gets its tier (`dispatcher.rs`)

A job's po2 is fixed before it reaches the dispatcher: it was measured at that po2, and a guest can tell the size it runs at. So the tier never changes a po2. It decides the budget figure, and the claim side's card size.
- **Claim side.** `WorkerPool::risc0_slots` reports an Apple GPU slot's figure as its tier's, so jobs claimed while the owner is active are measured and sized at the active po2.
  - That read may move the tier back to idle. That is safe: the card's po2 rises, and a job measured at the smaller po2 still proves at it (`risc0_prove_sizes` picks the largest measured size <= the card's).
  - It never moves the tier to active.
- **Job start (`prove_on_slot`).** The owner is read, and the tier may switch to active only here. `metal_sizing::job_tier` then picks:
  - no job po2 (benchmark-style proofs): idle;
  - a po2 the active tier cannot hold (e.g. a job measured at 19 just before the owner returned): idle;
  - owner active: active;
  - owner idle and the job fits both: the live worker's tier, so there is no respawn before the proof.
  - The worker is respawned at the tier's figure before the proof only when the live worker would prove the job otherwise (`metal_sizing::same_for_job`, fix round 1): enforcement on and another figure, or a figure that does not admit the po2, or another Groth16 key decision at that po2. This is the same path as the count recycle, forced even near the deadline when the old worker could not hold the po2. Unenforced (the default), a job the active tier admits proves the same on the idle worker, so no tier respawn happens at all.
- **After the proof.** The worker is moved to the owner's tier in the idle gap (section 3) only when the next job would otherwise make that respawn itself (`WorkerPool::idle_gap_tier`): to active only after a job whose po2 the active tier holds, to idle when the worker would prove a job at the idle po2 (or at the last job's) otherwise.
- **Record.** The `Proof complete` line carries `(Metal tier active, worker figure 4.0 GiB, po2 18)`: the job's tier (its po2 is that tier's) and the figure of the worker that proved it. `WorkerPool::metal_tier_jobs()` counts proofs per tier, and `Owner tier idle -> active (input idle 12 s, ...)` is logged at each switch.
- **Benchmark and pricing rows keep the idle rate.**
  - `benchmark_slot`, `benchmark_slot_streaming` and `calibrate_slot_po2` respawn a Metal worker at the idle figure first, if it ran at another (`metal_idle_tier_for_benchmark`). The worker sizes its suite from its spawn figure.
  - The device row (`metal_job_po2(vram_bytes)`), the benchmark deadline and routing read `WorkerEntry::vram_bytes`, which stays the idle figure.
  - The claim-side feasibility of a job sized at the active po2 already charges the step down (`risc0_conservative_cps` / `RISC0_PO2_STEP_DOWN_FACTOR` in run.rs), so the price stays the idle rate while the deadline check sees the slower size.
- The worker's `--benchmark` honours `ZKMINER_OWNER_ACTIVE=1` (`apply_planned_budget(true)`), which plans with the active figure. That is how the tier is measured below. An IPC worker never reads the variable: the dispatcher hands it the figure.

End to end through the dispatcher with the real worker (first round, `tier_ipc/`, `m10_recycled_first_prove` with a 16 GB figure, po2 18 jobs, fibonacci 100000):

The worker then logs `Metal working-set limit 4.0 GiB: default segment po2 18`, both proofs report `(Metal tier active, 4.0 GiB, po2 18)`, and the tier counts are (0, 2).

## 3. Respawn right after a proof (M9 open issue 4)

`WorkerPool::after_metal_proof` runs after every successful Metal `ProofResult`:
- **On the miner's pool** (`engine::worker_pool`), work moves to a background thread after the result is handed back, so a proof never waits for a respawn.
  - The thread takes the slot once the caller has released it (it polls for up to 2 s, and only while the same worker is there with no proof since).
  - If the worker is due by the count or by the tier, it is killed and respawned at once. The new worker's M9 first-job prewarm then runs in the idle gap.
  - Otherwise, about 2.25 s after the proof (once the footprint has settled), it makes the same growth-net check the next proof would make (`metal_idle_outgrown`, settled reading, at most one 0.25 s confirm). It respawns if the worker has outgrown its baseline.
  - A proof that takes the slot first makes its own checks, as before.
- **On any other pool** (tests), the count and tier checks run inline and the growth net is left to the next proof.
- `ZKMINER_METAL_RESPAWN_EARLY=0` restores M9's behaviour.

IPC A/B, real worker through the miner's pool (`m10_recycled_first_prove`, recycle after every proof, 3 Proves of fibonacci 400000 with a 3 s gap, default budget, po2 20), 8 interleaved rounds, load 2.1-14.3 (`ipc/r`):

| | off (M9: respawn before the next Prove) | on | on-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| proof 1 (fresh worker; the control) | 7.052 [7.038-7.066] s | 7.054 [7.049-7.067] s | 0.00 |
| **proof 2 (recycled worker's first Prove)**, wall | 7.206 [7.178-7.234] s | 7.051 [7.042-7.072] s | **-0.155 [-0.192, -0.109], 0/8 slower** |
| proof 3, wall | 7.217 [7.200-7.234] s | 7.053 [7.033-7.069] s | -0.167 [-0.191, -0.136], 0/8 |

- Both arms are the final code (the harness is new). "off" is `ZKMINER_METAL_RESPAWN_EARLY=0`, whose code path is M9's: `after_metal_proof` returns at once, and the pre-proof recycle is unchanged. That stands in for an unmodified-build A, which cannot run this test.
- The respawn itself took 0.160 s [0.130-0.180] (24 respawns). It is now off the critical path.
- The wall-time gain is the respawn. The worker's own timer also shows M9's prewarm gain, but part of it moves outside that timer, so in wall time a recycled worker's first proof now costs what a fresh one does.

## 4. Keccak

- **Charge decision: the proved po2.**
  - Since K10, `ProveKeccakRequest.po2` is `request_po2(inputs, max_po2)`: the smallest circuit that holds the batch, never above the session's max. The prover proves the request at exactly that po2 (`syscall/keccak.rs:132`).
  - `keccak_request_charge(po2) = KECCAK_REQUEST_CYCLES << (po2 - 17)` (floored at 17) therefore follows the proving work. A partial batch under `RISC0_KECCAK_PO2=18` that fits 17 costs a 17 proof and is charged 2^20, not 2^21.
  - Charging the requested max would overcharge every partial batch.
  - The test `the_keccak_charge_follows_the_proved_po2` pins it: 14-17 -> 2^20, 18 -> 2^21, and two capped 17s equal one 18.
- **Cross-build equality is not needed.** The measured cycles (segments' 2^po2 plus this charge) feed only this host's `estimated_cycles` (`max(declared, measured)`), i.e. pricing, collateral and deadline feasibility. No receipt, journal or other host compares them. They already differ across card sizes, because segments are padded to the card's po2.
- **The 4 GiB tier never requests a keccak po2 that cannot fit.**
  - The worker never sets `RISC0_KECCAK_PO2`, and the zkvm default max is 17.
  - W7-V2 (branch `metal/W7-V2`, not yet on `zkminer/metal`; its note was read) measured on the session path, which the worker uses (`prove_with_opts`). Keccak starts after `release_hal_buffers` with 29-45 MiB live, and po2 17 peaks at 3968-4094 MiB under a 4 GiB budget. Its eval chunk shrinks under an explicit budget. Unconstrained, it peaks at 4484 MiB.
  - The K10 "po2 17 fails at 4 GiB" was the harness's segment po2 19, which the worker's 4 GiB tier (po2 18) never uses.
  - Without enforcement, the active tier's HAL limit is the device's (10.67 GiB on a 16 GB Mac), so the 4484 MiB unconstrained keccak 17 peak fits there.
  - `RISC0_KECCAK_PO2=18` needs 7084 MiB (W7-V2). The worker now warns at start when that is set and its plan is smaller (`metal_host::keccak_po2_warning`).
  - W7-V2's open point stands: its headroom cap does not reach the `KeccakAudit` executor (coprocessor callback). The worker should therefore never set po2 18 on those tiers, which it does not.
- This Mac has no keccak guest in the worker, so no keccak proof was run here.

## Fix round 1 (review findings)

1. **36 GB: a tier switch respawned the worker and changed nothing.** At 28 and 20 GiB the worker proves po2 20 the same way: both ceilings admit it (21 at 28 GiB, 20 at 20 GiB), and the Groth16 key is released at both, since keeping it needs ~29.5 GiB. Unenforced, the figure reaches a worker only through its hard po2 ceiling, its Groth16 key decision and its default po2 (benchmarks, prewarm). Fix (the review's option a): `metal_sizing::same_for_job(a, b, po2, enforced)` says whether a worker started at figure `a` proves a job of `po2` as one at `b` would. `prove_on_slot` and the idle gap respawn only when it says no. The claim side keeps the idle figure where the tier claims at the same po2 (`claim_limit`). The default po2 is ignored for a job that names its po2: a warm worker whose prewarm ran at another size still beats a cold one. Per Mac (`a_tier_switch_changes_a_job_only_where_the_worker_would_prove_it_otherwise`, 16-128 GB, po2 14-21): unenforced, a switch never changes a job its active tier admits, so the default makes no tier respawn on any Mac. No A/B is needed for a respawn that no longer happens. `a_36_gb_macs_owner_switches_never_respawn_the_worker` (mock, six switches, one pid) and the real-worker run above (one pid) show it. With the old rule (`respawn = limit != spawned`, restored on purpose for a run) both that test and `a_metal_job_proves_at_the_owner_tier_its_po2_fits` fail.
2. **Owner return with queued idle-po2 jobs: two respawns per job.** After a po2 19 job the idle gap moved the worker to 4 GiB, and the next queued po2 19 job moved it back before its proof. Fix: `idle_gap_tier` moves a worker to the active tier only after a job whose po2 the active tier holds (the queued jobs share that size), and only when `same_for_job` says it differs. The first po2 18 job's start makes the one respawn instead. A count or growth recycle now keeps the worker's figure unless that rule moves it. The old code moved it to the owner tier on every recycle, which had the same defect. Test `queued_idle_po2_jobs_after_the_owner_returns_respawn_the_worker_at_most_once` (enforcement on, since otherwise nothing respawns at all): 3 queued po2 19 jobs give 0 respawns, the first po2 18 job gives 1, and the owner leaving gives 1 back in the idle gap (or at the po2 19 job's start with `ZKMINER_METAL_RESPAWN_EARLY=0`). It fails with the admits check removed from `idle_gap_tier` (checked on purpose, then restored).
3. **Claim/prove race.** It needs the prove step to size from the idle figure and the claim step from the tier's. Both call `current_risc0_cards` -> `WorkerPool::risc0_slots` in run.rs, which this item may change only in tests. The dispatcher cannot tell the two calls apart, and it cannot see the jobs queued in run.rs. So, as the review asks, `auto` no longer ships as the default: `ZKMINER_OWNER_ACTIVE` unset or `0` keeps the idle tier, and `auto` / `1` are opt-in (`metal_sizing::owner_force`, test `the_owner_tier_is_off_unless_asked_for`). The selector, the policy, the measurements and the tests stay, ready for when run.rs is fixed (open issue 3).

Non-blocking items:
- The duplicate `#[test]` on `the_keccak_charge_follows_the_proved_po2` is removed; the worker test build has no warning from this crate.
- The `apply_planned_budget` comments now say what is true: it runs before any proof or HAL runtime (the readers of the variables). rayon's idle pool and the Metal device opened by the support check already exist then, and neither reads the environment.
- `keep_groth16_cache` and its constants moved from `metal_host` to `metal_sizing`, which the worker includes by path. The host and the worker now share one copy of the decision `same_for_job` compares.
- `a_metal_job_proves_at_the_owner_tier_its_po2_fits` used to fail with `ZKMINER_METAL_RESPAWN_EARLY=0`. All tier tests now pass with the knob off (`gates2/wl_early0.log`).
- Two tier tests need distinct 4 and 8 GiB figures, so they return early under an explicit `RISC0_METAL_MAX_WORKING_SET_BYTES` (gpu-run's light default of 2 GiB caps both). The gate runs them with `--light --no-budget`, since the mock worker uses no GPU.
- The display-sleep precedence over battery is now documented in the policy (section 2).
- A reviewer reran two A/Bs in a second session and got smaller but still clear effects. Enforcement footprint: +0.382 GB [+0.371, +0.394] (claimed +0.476). Enforcement STARK: +0.50 s (+5 %, 7/7; claimed +0.55). Respawn-early: -0.178 s (claimed -0.155). The decisions stand.

## Knobs

- `ZKMINER_METAL_ENFORCE_BUDGET=1`: the worker enforces its plan as `RISC0_METAL_MAX_WORKING_SET_BYTES`. Default off (section 1).
- `ZKMINER_OWNER_ACTIVE=auto|1|0`: unset or `0` (default) keeps the idle tier; `auto` reads the owner; `1` forces the active tier. In `--benchmark`, `1` simulates the active tier.
- `ZKMINER_OWNER_IDLE_SECS` (300): the input-idle threshold. Back to idle takes twice it.
- `ZKMINER_METAL_RESPAWN_EARLY=0`: respawn a due worker before the next proof, as M9 did.
- Unchanged: `ZKMINER_METAL_PO2_TABLE=m1` (the tier table moves with it), `ZKMINER_METAL_RECYCLE_AFTER_PROOFS`, `ZKMINER_METAL_RECYCLE_GROWTH_BYTES`.

## Tried and rejected

- **Enforcing the planned budget by default** (the brief's item 1), at both tiers: slower and larger, numbers in section 1.
- **Switching the tier on every claim-side read** (`risc0_slots`): a job measured at 19 and proved after the switch would meet an 18 card and prove unmeasured. So reads only move the tier back to idle (see open issue 3).
- **A synchronous respawn at the end of `prove_on_slot`**: it would add ~0.16 s to the finished proof's result latency. It is background-only on the miner's pool.
- **Respawning a worker at the tier's figure whenever the figure differs** (first round): it cost a cold worker on every switch where nothing else changed (36 GB, and every unenforced Mac for jobs the active tier admits). It also cost two respawns per queued idle-po2 job when the owner came back. Replaced by `same_for_job` and `idle_gap_tier` (fix round 1).
- **`auto` as the default** (first round): see fix round 1, item 3.

## Open issues

1. **Real 16 GB Mac owner-active validation is deferred** (no hardware): browser plus IDE use, `vm_stat` swap and pageouts during proofs at both tiers, whether a 4 GiB tier keeps the Mac out of swap, and whether 300 s and its 2x band suit real use.
   - The display-sleep probe returned unknown in this session (`CGMainDisplayID` = 0); its effect was tested with the fake source only.
   - Battery and Low Power Mode could not be produced here.
2. **Enforcement.** Re-measure `ZKMINER_METAL_ENFORCE_BUDGET=1` after a risc0 change decouples the pool cap from the limit, or returns freed pages (`setPurgeableState(Empty)`, M9 open issue 2).
   - An untested middle ground is to enforce the device's own recommended limit: the same speed as today, with a typed refusal instead of over-commit at the device limit. It was not implemented: there is no measured benefit, and risc0 has "auto" modes keyed on the variable's presence (Z12 release, W7-V2's keccak rebuild).
3. **Claim/prove race at a switch to active (why `auto` is opt-in).**
   - With `auto` (or `1`), a job claimed and measured at the idle po2 that is still queued when another job's start switches the tier to active is offered the active card size at its prove step. `risc0_prove_sizes` then proves it at 18 unmeasured, with its existing warning, at a time planned for po2 19's rate. `run.rs::a_macs_risc0_slot_takes_the_owner_active_tier` pins the behaviour.
   - The fix is in run.rs, which this item may not change beyond tests. The prove step (`current_risc0_cards(state, false)`) should size cards from the idle figure (`WorkerPool::metal_tier_limit(.., Idle)`, or a new `risc0_slots` variant), and the claim step from `metal_sizing::claim_limit`. Once that lands, `auto` can be made the default again. The orchestrator should schedule it.
4. With `auto`, the first jobs after the owner returns still run at the idle tier when they were measured at the idle po2 (16 / 18 / 24 GB), and only jobs claimed after that are at the active po2. The lag is the queue's length.
5. The active-tier cost is large on small Macs: +37-39 % STARK on a 16 GB Mac while the owner is active. Pricing keeps the idle rate (as asked), so a long active period lowers real throughput below the row's price. The deadline side already charges the po2 step.
6. A calibrated Metal row (opt-in `zkminer benchmark --calibrate`) keeps a card at `max(calibrated, table default)` (`benchmark::risc0_slot_job_po2`, not owned here). With a row calibrated at 19, the active figure's default of 18 does not lower the card, so under `auto` the tier changes no claim on such a Mac. Default rows have no po2 samples and follow the figure.
7. `metal_host::KECCAK_PO2_18_MIN_LIMIT` (7084 MiB) copies W7-V2's measured minimum. It drives only a warning, but it should be taken from risc0's `KECCAK_MIN_PEAK_MIB` once W7-V2 merges.
8. Under `auto`, the owner reader links CoreGraphics into the miner and calls `CGMainDisplayID` / `CGDisplayIsAsleep` at each read. It was tested only from a terminal session (display: unknown). A LaunchAgent, daemon or no-WindowServer context is untested. With the default off, nothing is read.
9. After a Metal proof with a growth margin, the background thread's settled footprint reading and the next job's pre-proof reading both update the growth net's baseline tracker. The leak run shows the net still fires at the predicted proof count; the double reading has no test of its own.
